# FCAL paper

The current paper is [`FCAL_manuscript.pdf`](FCAL_manuscript.pdf). It is a
13-page IEEE-format research manuscript titled
*FCAL: Foreground-Constrained Attention Learning for Background-Robust
Classification*.

The editable LaTeX source, build files, and revision material are maintained
locally and are intentionally excluded from the public repository.

The manuscript is a research artifact. It does not imply journal acceptance,
and the ISIC subset is explicitly reported as a small non-clinical negative
check rather than evidence of clinical effectiveness.
